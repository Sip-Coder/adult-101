# Adult 101 (working title)

**One hub site, LMS-style, covering all the practical life skills school never taught — in one place.**

Adult 101 is a website and learning management system for young adults. Instead of hunting across dozens of blogs, videos, and forums, learners get structured courses, lessons, checklists, and quizzes on the real-world "adulting" topics nobody walks them through: money, housing, cars, legal basics, starting a business, and what to do when stuff breaks.

## Draft topic map

### 1. Legal & Relationships
- Prenups: what they are, who needs one, how they work
- Divorce basics: process, costs, custody, splitting assets
- Contracts and leases: reading the fine print
- Wills, powers of attorney, and beneficiaries
- Small claims court and knowing your rights

### 2. Money
- Personal finance fundamentals
- Budgeting and money management
- Paying bills: due dates, autopay, avoiding late fees
- Banking, checking vs. savings, emergency funds
- Credit scores, credit cards, and debt
- Student loans
- Taxes: W-2s, 1099s, filing a return
- Saving, investing, and retirement accounts (401k, IRA)

### 3. Housing
- Renting: searching, applications, deposits, tenant rights
- Renters insurance
- Buying a first home: pre-approval, down payments, closing
- Mortgages explained
- Property / homeowners insurance
- HOAs: fees, rules, and what to check before buying
- Utilities and setting up a new place

### 4. Vehicles
- Buying vs. leasing, new vs. used
- Car loans
- Car insurance: coverage types, deductibles, claims
- Basic car maintenance schedules
- Registration, DMV, and inspections
- What to do after an accident

### 5. Business & Entrepreneurship
- Starting a business: ideas to launch
- Business structures (sole prop, LLC, etc.)
- Licenses, permits, and EINs
- Side hustles and freelance taxes
- Basic bookkeeping and separating personal/business money

### 6. When Stuff Breaks
- Home repairs: DIY vs. calling a pro
- Car breakdowns and roadside emergencies
- Dealing with landlords, contractors, and customer service
- Insurance claims step by step
- Lost jobs, medical bills, and other financial emergencies
- Identity theft and scams

## Status
Early concept. Scope, audience, tone, and structure are being defined.
